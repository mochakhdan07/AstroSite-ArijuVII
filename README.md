AstroSite: Lunar Landing Site Navigator

An Interactive Platform for Lunar Landing Site Exploration

🚀 About the Project

AstroSite is a web-based platform developed to support the exploration and understanding of lunar landing sites.

The project focuses on lunar exploration by providing users with an interactive digital experience related to Moon landing locations and space exploration.

Developed as part of the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, AstroSite aims to make lunar exploration information more accessible and engaging.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Introduce lunar landing sites and Moon exploration.
- Provide an accessible digital exploration experience.
- Encourage interest in lunar science and space technology.
- Support interactive learning about lunar exploration.
